DCF valuation: was €8.00 a fair price for ICG?

A discounted cash flow model valuing ICG at 30 June 2026 and testing it against the buyout offer.

Approach

  • Unlevered free cash flow for H2 2026 to 2030.
  • WACC of 8.5%.
  • Terminal value two ways: 2% perpetuity growth and an 8.0x exit multiple.
  • Renewal capex set close to depreciation.

Results

Value per share
Perpetuity growth€9.32
8.0x exit multiple€7.65
Offer€8.00
Sensitivity range€7.10 to €13.71

My read

If ICG is worth what listed ferry companies trade on (DFDS 5.1x, Viking Line 7.8x), €8.00 is fair. If its routes and port assets deserve an infrastructure multiple, it looks cheap. The answer mostly depends on the terminal multiple and the cost of replacing the fleet.
