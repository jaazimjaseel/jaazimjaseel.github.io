Financial due diligence: Irish Continental Group

In July 2026 ICG’s management agreed to take the Irish Ferries owner private at €8.00 a share, about €1.47bn of enterprise value. I reviewed the published accounts the way an outside investor in the deal might: are the earnings, cash and debt behind that price what they look like?

What I found

  • LTM EBITDA of €154.6m overstates 2026. Peak-season car and freight volumes were falling, and my FY2026 estimate is €146.9m.
  • Maintenance capex (€19.4m) is about a third of PP&E depreciation (€53.7m), so reported free cash flow looks better than it will once the fleet needs renewing.
  • Working capital is negative and seasonal. June cash is €17.2m above normal, which I treat as debt-like.
  • Adjusted net debt is about €258m against €226m reported.
