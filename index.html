My story

I studied commerce in Bangalore and joined KPMG Global Services in 2022. Over three years I audited more than 50 UK companies, and the work that pulled me towards deals was the forward-looking part: testing cash-flow forecasts for going concern and taking working capital apart. In 2025 I moved to Dublin for an MSc in Finance at UCD Smurfit, where I built a DCF equity valuation. Now I want to do that work on the side that prices the deal.

Selected work

  2. 02 DCF valuation: was €8.00 a share a fair price? A discounted cash flow model tested against the buyout offer. Answer: €7.65 to €9.32 a share.
